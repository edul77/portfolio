# About

I'm a systems administrator and DevOps engineer who builds and operates reliable, secure infrastructure. My work sits at the intersection of systems administration, DevOps, and networking — provisioning servers, wiring up networks, and automating everything in between so environments stay stable and reproducible.

On the systems side I run Linux and Windows fleets, harden them, patch them, and keep them monitored. On the DevOps side I treat infrastructure as code, build CI/CD pipelines, and ship changes with confidence. On the networking side I design and troubleshoot the connectivity — DNS, routing, firewalls, VPNs, and load balancing — that ties it all together. Good documentation and sensible automation are what I rely on to turn a fragile setup into one that runs itself.
